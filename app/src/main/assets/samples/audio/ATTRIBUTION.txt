Demo audio samples

Dataset: Deepfake Audio Detection Dataset (v4)
Author: Gary Stafford
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/garystafford/deepfake-audio-detection
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Included source clips:
- demo_real.wav: real/yt_0000_part_001.flac (reference label: real)
- demo_fake.wav: fake/el_0001_part_001.flac (reference label: fake)
- 10 additional real clips from distinct source recordings in real/
- 10 synthetic Amazon Polly clips from distinct source recordings in fake/
- 10 additional synthetic ElevenLabs clips from distinct source recordings in fake/
- The additional clips and source paths are listed in dataset/MANIFEST.csv

Changes made for this application: both clips are distributed as 16 kHz,
mono, 16-bit PCM WAV files. The real clip was downmixed from stereo and
resampled from 44.1 kHz. The fake clip was converted from FLAC to WAV.
The 30 additional clips are also distributed as 16 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM
WAV files; source recordings were downmixed to mono and resampled when needed.
Original files are from dataset revision
fcf5344bb7f82b54b6b932291326d29750ef1e82.

These samples are for exercising the app's audio-loading and inference flow.
They are not a validation set, and the detector may predict either label.

The bundled knn_modelv2.onnx model was trained using one-second clips from
this dataset. It is an adaptation derived from the dataset under CC BY 4.0.

The current bundled lgbmv2.onnx model is an exploratory fit using
Android-matched features from 21 of the 30 WAVs in dataset/MANIFEST.csv.
Three clips from each source category were held out from model fitting and
tested in the Android app on an x86_64 emulator using NNAPI: 8/9 clips were
classified correctly. This small result is not a reliable estimate of
performance on other clips, sources, devices, or one-second live audio.
The holdout was also used to decide whether to promote the candidate, so it
is not an untouched final test. Bundled WAV analysis uses full clips (up to
six seconds), not one-second chunks. Training details are in
notebooks/DeepFakeDetector/train_lgbm_bundled_audio.py.

The previous LightGBM model was trained using the York feature data in
notebooks/DeepFakeDetector/audio_features_stringremoved.csv; its York
feature-row holdout score did not represent Android audio performance.
