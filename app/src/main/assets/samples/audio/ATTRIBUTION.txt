Demo audio samples

Dataset: Deepfake Audio Detection Dataset (v4)
Author: Gary Stafford
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/garystafford/deepfake-audio-detection
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Included source clips:
- demo_real.wav: real/yt_0000_part_001.flac (reference label: real)
- demo_fake.wav: fake/el_0001_part_001.flac (reference label: fake)

Changes made for this application: both clips are distributed as 16 kHz,
mono, 16-bit PCM WAV files. The real clip was downmixed from stereo and
resampled from 44.1 kHz. The fake clip was converted from FLAC to WAV.

These samples are for exercising the app's audio-loading and inference flow.
They are not a validation set, and the detector may predict either label.

The bundled knn_modelv2.onnx model was trained using one-second clips from
this dataset. It is an adaptation derived from the dataset under CC BY 4.0.

The bundled lgbmv2.onnx model was supplied separately by the application
author and corresponds to the "Final Audio LGBM Classifier" notebook. Its
reported notebook metrics are not representative of Android performance.
The Android chroma_stft input is approximated and has not been validated
against the notebook's feature extraction pipeline.
